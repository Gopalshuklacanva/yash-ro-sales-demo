# Yash RO Sales: Design Preview

Static, responsive website demo for Yash RO Sales in Panki, Kanpur.
This is an independent design proposal, not the business's official website.

## Hosting

- Serve the repository root as a static directory.
- Entry point: `index.html`.
- No dependencies, build command, database or environment variables are needed.
- Keep `noindex, nofollow`, `robots.txt` and the independent-preview footer until
  the business approves an official launch.
- Choose a hosting plan that permits the intended commercial use.

## Content

Business details, 5.0 rating and 80 reviews were checked on Google Maps on
27 September 2026. These are a dated snapshot, not a live feed.
The gallery uses six owner-posted photos or graphics. The hero is an AI-edited
transparent cutout derived from one of those photos.

Business photos and review excerpts retain their original ownership. Obtain
the business owner's approval for ongoing official use. Font licensing is
included in `assets/manrope-license.txt`.

## Local Check

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this directory with a local static server.
Check JavaScript syntax with `node --check site.js`.
